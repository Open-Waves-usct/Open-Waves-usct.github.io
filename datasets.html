Breast Dataset

OpenWaves includes 8,000 breast phantoms designed to represent the distribution of diverse human breast types. As shown in Table 1, the dataset is divided into four density categories:

  • Heterogeneous (HET): Complex tissue composition
  • Fibroglandular (FIB): Dense fibrous tissue
  • All Fatty (FAT): Predominantly adipose tissue
  • Extremely Dense (EXD): High glandular density
Breast Type Frequency (kHz) #Train/#Test # Source Storage

Fibroglandular (FIB) 300~650 2700/300 256 10.8TB
Fatty (FAT) 300~650 1800/200 256 7.2TB
